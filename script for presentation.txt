But Videos isn't everything, how about your one-drive, We all saw how Stefan's one drive was full of Duplicates, But in our opinion a live one-drive of an employee might be the worst thing to poke around in. We don't know anything about what they want in there and what they are working on right now. And i don't want to think about what the consecvences of deleting the wrong thing could be.

So we instead focused on another problem, what happens when someone changes computers, quits or the real gold mine, retire. Our solution starts when IT department snapshots their system and now unused one drive.
We search it all looking for Pictuers,Powerpoints and PDFs and by hashing these files and comparing hashes, same = discard/delete, but different hashes could still mean Powerpoints and PDFs share the same Slides and Pages and are just different versions. So we Hash every slide and page, A Powerpoint with 80% Similar slides as another means we only have to save the 20% difference, think of version control. 

But we went even deeper, photos, Big companies spend big money on photos, logos and their brand and copying is always easier than creating new, that's why we disassemble each slide we save looking for photos to take out and store, leaving a reference with the size and location save even more space. Taking what would be a MB slide into Bytes to store
